Liftlog support

Liftlog is a no-frills gym notebook for iPhone. Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome at txm_support@icloud.com.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. It is included in your iCloud or computer backup, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings your notebook with it. See the privacy policy.

Can I get my workouts out of the app?
Yes. Settings › Export CSV creates a spreadsheet file of all your sets that you can save, email or open in Numbers or Excel.

Why doesn't the rest timer show on my lock screen?
Check Settings › Liftlog on your iPhone and make sure Live Activities and Notifications are on.

Can I switch between kg and lb?
Yes, in Liftlog's Settings. Weights you logged in the other unit are converted for display.

I made a typo in an exercise name.
Rename it from the exercise's ⋯ menu in a workout, or under Settings › Exercises. The new name is used everywhere, including your history.
