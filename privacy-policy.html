Liftlog privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Liftlog is a workout notebook for iPhone. It is built so that your information never leaves your phone.

What Liftlog collects

Nothing. Liftlog has no account, no server and no network code. The developer does not receive, see or store any of your data.

What stays on your iPhone

The workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes and ratings you enter are stored only on your iPhone, together with your settings (weight unit and rest time). Like the rest of your phone's data, they are included in your own iCloud or computer backup if you have one turned on. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Liftlog uses them only to tell you when a rest timer ends. These are local notifications created on your iPhone; nothing is sent over the internet.

Export

When you choose Export CSV, Liftlog creates a file and opens the iOS share sheet. Where the file goes from there is your choice.

Tracking, ads and third parties

Liftlog has no ads, no analytics and no third-party code, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Liftlog collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
